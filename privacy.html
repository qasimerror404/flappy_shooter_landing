Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Flappy Shooter ("we", "us", "the app") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

What we collect

  • Account info — if you sign in with Google: your display name, profile photo URL, and Google account ID. We do not see your password.
  • Gameplay data — your best score, total runs, and a per-user document on our leaderboard.
  • Diagnostic data — anonymized crash reports and basic usage analytics (game starts, game overs) via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics.
  • Push token — if you opt in to notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token tied to your install.

What we don't collect

  • We do not collect contacts, location, microphone, camera, or any other device data.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Where data lives

All cloud data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Crashlytics, Analytics, Cloud Messaging). Firebase is operated by Google LLC; see Firebase Privacy Disclosures.

Your choices

  • Play as a guest — anonymous sign-in is automatic; no personal info is shared.
  • Turn notifications off — Profile → Settings → Notifications.
  • Reset progress — clears local stats. Profile → Settings → Reset Progress.
  • Delete your account — permanently removes your leaderboard entry and Firebase Auth record. Profile → Settings → Delete Account.

Children

The app is rated for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.
